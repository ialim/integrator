## Read of the document

**Purpose and audience.** This document exists to help architects, story writers, the founder, and an accountant or counsel find the technical detail, edge-case guards, breakeven inputs, and regulatory research behind the PRD without weighing down the PRD's main narrative.

**Structure model: Reference/Database.** Readers jump to one item (a status list, a register row, a cost input, a legal note) instead of reading front to back, so the tests are random access, mutually exclusive topics, and a consistent schema. The schema holds: the register tables share one four-column layout, and the other sections lead each item with a bold run-in label. The shape fights the purpose in three ways, which rows 1, 2, 5, and 13 address: there is no map, the 13 H2 sections interleave audiences (technical notes, founder decisions, legal research), and the two longest sections (Breakeven model, 1,314 words; Edge-case register, 2,444 words, 41% of the file) have no subheadings. Real redundancy with the PRD is small: two sections (rows 10 and 12) and a few sentences.

**Voice noted and preserved.** Telegraphic imperative fragments in the register ("Refuse", "Block with a list"); British spelling (licence, fulfilment, harbour, normalise); ranges written with "to"; "about" for estimates; Title Case Glossary terms; [S] and [I] evidence tags; bold run-in labels. The prose rows leave these alone and fix only ambiguity, grammar, Glossary terms, and overlong sentences.
