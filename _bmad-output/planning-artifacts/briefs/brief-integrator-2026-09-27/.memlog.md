- (note by user) Core concept: platform lets integrators design projects room-by-room from a wholesale catalogue (sourced from multiple prospective suppliers), share client-facing proposals with their own margin included, confirm order only once integrator pays; also supports direct-buy without design step
- (note by user) Also offers install/solution tutorials + documentation, tiered reseller discounts by loyalty, and discounted back-order for catalog items not held in stock
- (decision by user) Vertical: broad systems-integrator definition (IT, AV, security, home-automation, industrial automation, electrical) rather than one niche
- (decision by user) Greenfield: platform and supplier/distribution network both being built from scratch together, no pre-existing wholesale business to bootstrap from
- (decision by user) Form factor: web platform; mobile/field access matters (integrators in the field), but no on-site room-measurement tooling required
- (decision by user) Geography: launching focused on Nigeria and West Africa
- (decision by user) No end-client login/accounts in MVP; B2C option (clients log in, optionally hire an integrator through the platform) is a parked future idea, not in scope
- (decision by user) Starting wedge vertical: IT/AV/security/home-automation (residential + light commercial). Industrial automation and complex commercial (multi-resident, hotels, office complexes) is long-term vision, not MVP
- (decision by user) Stakes: bootstrapped/personal venture now; investor/partner pitch is a live future possibility, so brief should carry enough rigor to survive that use later
- (decision by user) Loyalty discount tiers: trailing-window spend model (not lifetime cumulative) so tiers can move up or down with recent activity
- (event) Grounding research done: (1) global/US comparable-platform landscape (ADI, D-Tools, Snap One, iPoint, WeQuote) confirms catalog+design+quoting+procurement is normally stitched together via integrations, not native-unified -- real whitespace; (2) Nigeria/West Africa regional research confirms the specific verticals (AV/security/IT/industrial/electrical wholesale) are greenfield with no incumbent platform, fragmented trade associations, severe naira volatility (~460 to ~1330-1430 in 2 years) making pricing strategy a first-class design decision, counterfeit risk ~40% in some categories, and emerging embedded trade-credit models (TradeDepot/Omnibiz/Moniepoint) rather than standalone credit infra
- (decision by user) Revenue model: purely margin-on-goods (buy from suppliers, mark up, sell wholesale to integrators) -- no separate platform fee, subscription, or take-rate
- (decision by user) Pricing/currency strategy: deliberately price around naira volatility (dollar-indexed / actively managed pricing) rather than holding fixed naira prices and absorbing FX risk
- (decision by user) Go-to-market / cold-start plan: bootstrap initial catalog from founder's own existing supplier contacts, and initial integrator customers from founder's own integrator network
- (decision by user) Primary year-one success signal: repeat-purchase rate (integrators coming back to buy again), not just signups or one-time GMV
- (note by user) 'integrator' is a working title, not a finalized product/brand name
