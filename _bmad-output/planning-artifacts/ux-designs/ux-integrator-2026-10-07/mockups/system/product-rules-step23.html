The rules only this product has

Proposal for your reaction. Steps 1 to 22 drew the screens and the general patterns. This sheet writes down five rules that cut across all of them and that no general design guide would give us: how money is shown, how time is counted, who sees what, how we prove it is you, and what still works with no signal. Each row says which PRD requirement it protects, so nothing here changes the product. It only says how the screens behave. Nothing is decided until you confirm it.

How the code behaves

  • It is asked for at the moment of the action, never at sign-in and never before the screen opens.

  • It goes to a contact on record, partly hidden: “We sent a code to 0803 •••• 5678.”

  • Six boxes over one real input, as at sign-in. Pasting works. It says how long is left. One action per code

  • After the right code, the action finishes in the same step. Nothing is asked twice.

  • If it does not come: Send again after the countdown, or Call or WhatsApp us.

  • Staff sign in with a second factor and a 12-hour session. The console warns 5 minutes before it ends.

5. Offline and weak signal

The PRD gives offline a narrow job: design a Project on site and reach the guides. Anything that commits money needs a connection and says so. This section adds nothing to that scope.

Works with no signal

  • Browse and search the Catalogue as it was when the phone last synced.

  • Make and change Projects, rooms, products and quantities.

  • Show the parts list on screen.

  • Open saved manuals, guides and PDFs. Videos only if they were saved on request.

  • See Saved on this phone: what is waiting, and the day to check in by.

Needs a connection, and says why

  • Make a Proposal. “Prices must be current. Connect to make a Proposal.”

  • Use the cart or create an order. “An order is made at today’s price.”

  • Orders, payments, receipts and credit notes. “We check these against the bank.” They are not kept on the phone.

  • Sign in for the first time, any code, any change in Account. “We need to reach you to do this.”

Saved projects and the 14-day check-in

  1. Wed 7 Oct · day 0The phone checks in. Saved projects open.
