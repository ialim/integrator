Step 22 of the walk-through

Responsive and platform: the app on a laptop, and the rules around it

Proposal for your reaction. You said the integrator app is for phones and for desktop browsers, and the staff console is desktop only. Everything so far has been drawn on a phone, so this step does two things: it draws the app at laptop width, for the four screens that change most, and it sets down the platform rules, covering install, browsers, weak data and speed. Same data and same words as Steps 1 to 6. Nothing here is decided until you confirm it.

1. Three sizes

One product, one set of screens, and the layout adapts. The screen names, words and order of things never change with size.

SizeWhat changes
Phone
up to 599 px wide		The screens you have confirmed. One column, the tab bar along the bottom, sheets that rise from the bottom, a 16 px margin. Works down to 320 px wide.
Tablet
600 to 1023 px		The phone layout, centred, in a column up to 720 px wide. The tab bar stays. Nothing is stretched. On a tablet held wide, lists and a chosen item can sit side by side from 900 px.
Laptop and desktop
1024 px and up		A left rail replaces the tab bar. The cart sits at the top right. Content stops at 1100 px, centred. Lists with an item behind them become a list and a detail side by side (Orders, Projects, Credit notes). The Project page gets a summary panel that stays in view. Sheets become centred dialogs. A card list with more than four facts a row becomes a table.
Every sizeTouch, mouse and keyboard all work. Nothing needs a hover. Portrait and landscape both work, and neither is locked. The reading order never changes with the layout.

6. Platform, install and weak data

TopicWhat we propose
Browsers and phonesThe last two versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, and Samsung Internet. Android 9 or newer with Chrome. iPhone and iPad on iOS 16.4 or newer for the installed app. The test phone is the PRD's: a low-end Android with 2 GB of memory on a slow 3G link (400 kbps, 400 ms).
InstallingThe app installs from the browser and opens full screen, with its own icon and the green top bar. It is offered after the second visit, or right after the first "Save for offline", and always under Account. On an iPhone the steps are written in words: Share, then Add to Home Screen.
A new versionA new version is fetched quietly. It never reloads the page under someone's hands. A banner says "A new version is ready. Update when you finish what you are doing", and updating keeps what was typed.
iPhone and saved filesAn iPhone may clear what a website has saved if it is not opened for about a week. An installed app is not cleared that way. So on an iPhone, "Save for offline" asks the person to install first, and says why in one line. The app also asks the phone to keep its storage.
NotificationsNone from the browser in version 1. Messages go by WhatsApp and email, as decided in Step 10.
Speed and sizeThe Catalogue list shows within about 5 seconds on the test phone, as the PRD asks. Only system fonts, so no font is downloaded. Pictures load when they are near the screen, as small versions first. Lists load 20 at a time.
Data SaverWhen the phone's Data Saver is on, pictures wait until they are tapped, and videos never load by themselves.
The clockEvery countdown uses our clock, not the phone's, so a wrong phone clock cannot extend a price hold. Times are shown in West Africa Time.
Sharing and copyingSharing a code or a PDF uses the phone's own share sheet, with a Copy button as the fallback. Copy buttons work without special permission.
PrintingThe Parts list, the proposal, and every document are PDFs that print cleanly. Parts lists carry no prices.

7. One thing the PRD does not decide

What do we measure about use?

The success measures come from orders and projects, not from watching screens. Whether to count taps and page views as well, and what the Privacy Notice must then say, is not in the PRD. Proposed: nothing that follows a person, no advertising or tracking cookies, and only what the Privacy Notice lists, written with counsel. This is for you to decide before launch.

The console below 1024 px

As decided in Step 21, the console reflows when it is zoomed or the window is narrow. It is still not a phone product, and a phone showing it gets a notice that the console is for a desktop.
