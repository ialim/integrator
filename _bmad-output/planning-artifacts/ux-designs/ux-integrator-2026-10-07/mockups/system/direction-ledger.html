Ledger

Ledger presents the product as a careful institution: warm paper, deep ledger green, a serif for headings and hairline rules in place of boxes. It is the densest of the four directions, so most of a room and its totals sit in one view, and every amount is right-aligned in a tabular column that can be added up by eye. Status is a small mark plus a plain word, and escalation is a rule plus a label, never colour alone.

Established, exact and quiet: a private-bank ledger set for a phone.

Hero screen: Project, three variants

Phone frames are 360 px wide and shown at full scroll length. The amber notches on the bezel mark 780 px, the bottom of a typical phone screen.

Checks for this direction

Lowest body-text contrast: 7.05:1 (caution text #6E4400 on caution tint #F6EACB), against the 4.5:1 AA minimum for normal text. Every text and background pairing in this file was computed from the final styles. Lowest control-border contrast: 3.96:1 (control border #7A7258 on aged paper #F0E9D6); lowest icon contrast: 6.37:1; both against the 3:1 minimum for UI components.

Touch targets on the phone frames are at least 44 x 44 px (stepper buttons 46 x 44, section toggles 48 x 48). Every status carries its word, and most also carry an icon or mark. The focus ring is a 3 px ledger-green outline with a 2 px gap (11.19:1 on paper); it turns white on green bars. Light mode only.
