Step 11 of the walk-through

The Technician on site: invitation, a saved project, the guide, and sync

Proposal for your reaction, in the Direction A look. This is UJ-5 with Chidi, a Technician on Tunde's team, standing in a client's building with no signal. Six phone screens, then a sheet of states. A Technician sees no money anywhere, so these screens simply have no price areas rather than empty ones. The offline "Saved on this phone" screen is for everyone, not only Technicians. Wording is placeholder where it is not quoted from the PRD, and the guide text is a sample. Nothing here is decided until you confirm it.

States

Brass cards need something from someone. Red cards mean it did not work. Plain cards are information. Wording is a placeholder.

What a Technician never sees

No price areas

The layout has no gaps where money would be

Rows, cards and the catalogue are drawn without a price column, not with a blank one. No "as of" time, no discount tags, no totals, no sort or filter by price.

Back-order

He sees the flag and the wait, not the discount

"Back-order · about 3 weeks" shows. The back-order discount does not.

Orders tab

Status and deliveries, no money

As in Step 6: status, each delivery, serial numbers and guides. No amounts, payments, bank details or documents.

Cart and buying

Not there at all

No cart icon, no "Buy parts", no "Create order". A link to an order or payment page opens "This is for the Owner and Managers. Ask Tunde."
