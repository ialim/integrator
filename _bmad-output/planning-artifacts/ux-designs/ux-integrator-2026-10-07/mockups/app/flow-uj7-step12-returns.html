Step 12 of the walk-through

Returns: report a problem, send a device back, and the outcome

Proposal for your reaction, in the Direction A look. This is UJ-7: Chidi finds a faulty camera, Support gives him an issue code, he raises a return request, Funmi (Tunde's Manager) approves it, the device goes back, and the outcome arrives. Seven phone screens, two reference tables, then a sheet of states. Support comes first by phone or WhatsApp, as the PRD says, so the app's job is to record, guide and show status, and never to decide. A Technician sees no amounts anywhere on a return. Wording is placeholder where it is not quoted from the PRD. Nothing here is decided until you confirm it.

How long he has, and what can happen

The windows run from the courier-confirmed delivery date, or the collection date, and are met by the time of the first Case. The remedies are the PRD's. Admin can change the windows, and a change never affects a Case already open.

What he reportsHow long he hasWhat can happen
Damaged on the way, or the wrong item5 daysA replacement from stock, otherwise a credit note.
Does not work out of the box30 days, or the maker's window if shorterA replacement, then a repair by the maker, then a credit note.
Stopped working under warrantyThe warranty period, counted from dispatchThe maker's repair or replacement process only. Never a cash refund. The maker's reason is shown if it declines.
I think it is fakeThe warranty period plus 90 daysOnce confirmed, a refund by bank transfer. If cleared, the device goes back to him.
I changed my mind7 daysA credit note less a 15% restocking fee, for sealed, stocked items only.

Brass cards need something from someone. Red cards mean it did not work. Plain cards are information. Wording is a placeholder.

Outcomes

Credit note (Owner or Manager)

A credit note instead

"CN-0014 · ₦4,500.00: your courier cost. Open credit notes." The amounts show to Owners and Managers only. A Technician sees "a credit note was issued".

Waiting for the maker

The maker is repairing it

We cannot say when. We tell you when it is back. If the maker declines a warranty claim, the maker's reason is shown. There is no cash refund for a warranty claim.

Rejected

We could not accept this return

The reason is shown in plain words, for example "opened or installed goods cannot be returned for a change of mind". Call or WhatsApp us to talk it through.

Suspected counterfeit

Do not install or sell this device

Once confirmed, we refund by bank transfer. If cleared, we send it back. A recall notice reaches everyone affected and stays on Home until someone taps "I have seen this".

Other kinds of claim

I changed my mind (Owner or Manager)

Support gives you a return authorisation

The screen lists the rules: within 7 days, sealed stocked items, 15% restocking fee. An excluded item is refused with the reason shown, for example "back-order items cannot be returned for a change of mind".

It did not arrive (Owner or Manager)

Opened when a delivery is lost or overdue

No issue code. The answer is a new dispatch or a refund. A lost delivery opens it for you, with a message.
