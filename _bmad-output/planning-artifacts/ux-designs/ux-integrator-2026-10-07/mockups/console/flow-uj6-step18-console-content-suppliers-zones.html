Step 18 of the walk-through

The staff console: documentation, suppliers, stores and delivery zones

Proposal for your reaction, in the Direction A look. Same console shell. This step finishes the console: the guides a technician reads on site and how they are written, the suppliers behind the catalogue and who approves them, and where and for how much goods are delivered. Four desktop frames, then a sheet of states. Rates, estimates, addresses and supplier names are placeholders. Wording is placeholder where it is not quoted from the PRD. Nothing here is decided until you confirm it.

States

Brass cards need something from someone. Red cards mean it did not work or is blocked. Plain cards are information. Wording is a placeholder.

Documentation

What a business sees

"Documentation coming soon."

On a product with none, in the PRD's words. The Support line "Need it now? Call or WhatsApp us" sits under it, as decided in Step 11.

Replacing

Saved copies on 6 phones will say "Replaced"

Said before you save, so no one is surprised. The old version is kept, can be compared, and can be restored as a new version.

Removing

Saved copies will say "Withdrawn"

A reason is required, because a withdrawn guide may be unsafe. The people who saved it are told at their next sync.

A file that cannot be used

Not a PDF or a video we can play, or too big

Said in one line before it is saved. The size limits are placeholders until the build.

A guide that cannot be published

Step 4 has no text, or a photo with no description

Publish is off, and the step is marked. A guide needs at least one step, every step needs text, and every photo needs a description for people who cannot see it.

Copy from another product

Starts a new guide from an existing one

Useful for variants of the same product. It is a copy, so editing one never changes the other.

Suppliers

Missing terms

Sentinel Imports has no counterfeit-chargeback term

Shown on the list in brass. Its products stay on sale, and Admin is asked to get the term written down. A new product from it cannot go on sale until it is.

A bad switch

A supplier with no cost, or a cost of zero, is refused

The Active Supplier cannot be removed without a replacement. The preview names the products affected.

Who can do what

Operations proposes and enters costs, Admin approves and switches

Operations sees the Approve and Switch buttons with a lock and "Admin only". A switch needs a second approver and appears in Approvals.

Stopping a supplier

Not in the PRD: what happens to its products

Proposed: suspending a supplier takes its products off sale after a preview of what that touches, and a replacement supplier can be named per product. Needs your say.

Stores and delivery zones

Closing a store

6 shipments still point to this store

It cannot be switched off until they are reassigned, with a message to the businesses. The Main Store needs a replacement first.

Overlapping areas

Eti-Osa is in two zones

An address must resolve to exactly one zone. The save is refused and names the two zones. An area in no zone is listed as unplaced.

An address nobody covers

"We could not place this address"

When someone makes an order, they can pick a zone themselves, or ask for a delivery price from Operations. The second one creates a task here, and needs a place and a target in the console.

A flat rate

One price a shipment, however heavy

The PRD notes this loses money on racks, UPS units and displays. Weight bands are for a later version. Extra courier cost after payment is absorbed and reported.

Opening hours

Shown to a business choosing Pick-up

With the address. A closed day, like a public holiday, is shown from the Business Hours calendar.
