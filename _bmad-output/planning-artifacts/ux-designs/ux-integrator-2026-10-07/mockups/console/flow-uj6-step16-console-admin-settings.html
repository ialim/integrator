Step 16 of the walk-through

The staff console, Admin: the two-person rule, rules, staff, the audit trail, and the dashboard

Proposal for your reaction, in the Direction A look. Same console shell as before. This step holds the controls that protect the platform's money and its rules: changes that need a second person, the rules Admin can change, the staff and their deputies, the record that cannot be edited, the founder's weekly review, and the dashboard of success measures. Six desktop frames, then a sheet of states. Wording is placeholder where it is not quoted from the PRD. Nothing here is decided until you confirm it.

States

Brass cards need something from someone. Red cards mean it did not work or is blocked. Plain cards are information. Wording is a placeholder.

The two-person rule

Your own request

You asked for this, so someone else must approve it

The Approve button is off and says why. The same rule holds whatever the role: no one approves their own change.

Rate change above 10%

Refused as a probable typing error

Only Admin can override, with a reason. The override is marked for the weekly review.

Bank details change

Admin edits, a person in Finance approves, and it starts after 24 hours

Admin and Finance both get an email. Businesses see "Bank details changed on [date]" for 7 days. An order keeps the bank details it showed when it was made.

Switching the Active Supplier

Shows how many carts and projects it touches

A second approver is needed. A switch to a supplier with no cost, or a cost of zero, is refused.

Bulk price file

A cost change above 15%, or prices changed on more than 20 products

Lands here as a pending change with a dry-run report. Approving applies it, and the previous state is kept for a one-click rollback.

Serial exception

Operations and Admin both, and never the opener

Shows the label and box photos and the order line. Both names are recorded.

Declined

The reason goes back to the person who asked

They see it on their Today page. A declined item can be asked for again as a new request.

Rules and staff

Floor

This would take 3 products below the floor

The save is refused and names the three products and how far each falls.

Return windows

A change applies only to cases opened afterwards

Open cases keep the windows they started with. The screen says so before saving.

Tolerances and amounts

The underpayment tolerance and the second-approver amount need two people

Any write-off inside the tolerance is audited. The placeholders are ₦200.00 and ₦100,000.00 until the founder sets them. The size of a stock adjustment that needs an Admin to sign it off is set in the same group, placeholder 10 units.

Create a staff account

Name, work email, role, duties, deputy

They get an invitation. Two-step sign-in is set up at first sign-in, and nothing works until it is.

Last Admin

The last active Admin cannot be switched off

The button is off and says so. Changing a role away from Admin is refused for the same reason.

Out of office

Your queue moves to your deputy within a minute

Their actions are marked "acting for you". A banner on every screen reminds you it is on.

Terms, first businesses, and records

Publishing a new Terms version

Version, start date, full text, and a plain what-changed list

The list of 2 to 5 sentences is required, because the Owner reads it before accepting. A preview shows the Owner's screen. From the start date, orders need the Owner's acceptance. Older versions stay on file.

Seeding the first businesses

Make a business directly, and platform vouch codes

Name, contact and owner, and Admin who made it is recorded. It stays Pending Activation until the owner verifies, passes the NIN check, gives a trade name and accepts the Terms.

Reports and exports

Finance exports, as CSV

Orders, payments, credit notes with their ageing, the monthly VAT output that reconciles to the tax invoices, and the bank reconciliation. Finance and Admin can use it.

Dashboard

Each tile says where its number comes from

A tile that cannot be worked out says "Not enough data yet", never a zero. Percentages always sit beside their counts.

Unusual pattern alert

Many NIN reads, or many credit notes, by one person

Appears on Today for Admin and in the weekly review, with the person and the count.
