Step 19 of the walk-through

Voice and tone: how integrator speaks

Proposal for your reaction. This is the cross-cutting layer that makes the 18 steps you have confirmed sound like one product: six rules for the voice, the tone for each moment, the formats we always use, the word list, patterns for common messages, and a register of the words that must be checked by counsel or the accountant before launch. It covers the app, the staff console, WhatsApp and email messages, and the PDFs. Every example is taken from a screen you have already confirmed, or from the PRD's own words, which we never change. Nothing here is decided until you confirm it.

1. The voice in six rules

The reader is a working integrator in Nigeria, often on a phone, often in a hurry, sometimes standing on a ladder. The voice is plain, steady and respectful.

1. Plain words, short sentences

SayWe could not confirm your payment.
Do not sayPayment verification unsuccessful.

2. Say what happened, why, and what to do next

SayThis code has expired. Codes work for 48 hours. Ask the person who gave it to you for a new one.
Do not sayInvalid code.

3. A button says what it does

SaySend receipt. Ask for a refund. Put on hold.
Do not saySubmit. OK. Confirm. Proceed.

4. Stay calm about clocks and money

SayPrice held for 3 h 12 min. Send your receipt before 8 Oct, 10:42 am.
Do not sayHurry! Offer ending soon!

5. Never blame, never joke

SayThat code is not right. Check the 6 digits and try again.
Do not sayOops! You typed the wrong code.

6. One name for one thing

SayCredit note, every time, everywhere.
Do not sayCredit, balance, wallet, store credit, for the same thing.

No exclamation marks and no emoji anywhere. No "Oops", no "Sorry" for something routine, no idioms that do not travel. Sentence case everywhere: capitals only on the small label above a heading.

5. Patterns for common messages

Each pattern has a fixed shape so a new screen can be written without a meeting.

A button

A verb and, when it helps, the object. The primary button is the only filled one on a screen. A button that opens a dialog ends in an ellipsis. A button that cannot be pressed stays visible, looks dashed, and the reason is written next to it.

ExampleSend receipt · Ask for a refund · Put on hold… · Approve and send to Support

An error

Four short parts, in this order: what happened, why if we know, what to do, and where to get help. It never shows a code or a technical word.

ExampleWe could not confirm your payment. We could not match your transfer of ₦800,000.00 to our bank statement. Check the amount and the reference on your receipt. Need help? Call or send us a WhatsApp message.

A success

One line with the result, and one with what happens next. No celebration.

ExampleVouch code made for Hammed Bello. It works once, only for him, until Fri 9 Oct, 4:10 pm.

An empty place

Says why it is empty and offers the single next step. Never a cartoon.

ExampleNo projects yet. Make your first one: a name is all we ask.

Waiting and loading

Nothing for the first second. After that, words, and a bar with a percentage for anything that takes long, like a receipt upload. Never a spinner on its own.

ExampleSending, 62%. Waiting for signal.

A confirmation dialog

The title is the question and names the thing. The body lists what will happen. The buttons are the verb and Cancel. Red is for something that cannot be undone.

ExamplePut Sade AV Systems on hold? They cannot make new orders. They cannot be paid a refund. Payments for orders already made are still processed.

A role says no

Names who can do it. Never "Access denied". The row or button a role can never use is not shown at all.

ExampleOnly the Owner can ask for a refund.

No signal

Says what still works, how fresh it is, and that changes will send later.

ExampleNo signal. You are working from what is saved on this phone, checked today 7:10 am. Changes sync when you are back online.

A WhatsApp or email message

Names the order, proposal or case first. Says what happened, then what to do. One link. Under 300 characters on WhatsApp. Signed "integrator". A Technician's message never carries an amount. The PRD's own literals are never reworded.

To an OwnerOrder INT-0042: payment of ₦731,729.94 confirmed. We are checking stock and will approve it today. integrator

A message to a Technician

The same shape, with the money left out and the thing the person can do kept.

To a TechnicianDelivery 1 of order INT-0042 is on its way. Courier partner, tracking CP-88213. integrator
