1. How the app is organised

Six rules, all taken from the steps you have already confirmed. They are the information architecture in one place.

Five tabs, always in this order

Home, Catalogue, Projects, Orders, Account. Before approval there are only two: Catalogue and Get approved. A Technician has the same five, with Orders showing status and no amounts. A role sees only the tabs it can use.

The cart is not a tab

It is an icon with a count in the top bar, for Owners and Managers only. A Technician and an Owner who is not yet approved have none. On a laptop it moves to the top right.

One home for each thing

A thing lives in one tab. Other places link to it and never copy it. The table below says where.

Help is always the same place

“Call or WhatsApp us” is the last block of every screen and a row in Account. The Help sheet is where a person gives Support a code.

On a laptop the tab bar becomes a rail

Lists with an item behind them become a list and a detail side by side: Orders, Projects, Credit notes. The order of reading never changes.

The console is organised by work

A left rail in groups (Queues, Work, Money, Catalogue, People, Rules, Watch). Each role sees only its own groups. Today comes first. A queue opens as a list with a preview, and the full page opens from it.
