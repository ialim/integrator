Step 15 of the walk-through

The staff console, Admin: people and access

Proposal for your reaction, in the Direction A look. Same console shell as Steps 13 and 14. Admin can do everything, so the rail shows everything, but the Today page puts first what only Admin can decide: approving a business (UJ-2), checking an identity by hand, acting on an account, and deciding a return when Operations investigated it (UJ-7). Five desktop frames, then a sheet of states. Settings, staff, the audit trail and the founder's dashboard come in the next step. Wording is placeholder where it is not quoted from the PRD. Nothing here is decided until you confirm it.

States

Brass cards need something from someone. Red cards mean it did not work or is blocked. Plain cards are information. Wording is a placeholder.

Approving a business

Voucher suspended meanwhile

A brass banner across the top. Approve is off until you tick "I have seen this and still want to approve", and say why.

Rejecting

The reason is shown to the Owner

The Reject button opens a box for a reason in plain words. He sees it on his Get approved screen. A reason is recorded on every decision, approvals too.

A vouchee later suspended for fraud

The voucher's vouching rights are suspended pending review

Their waiting codes stop working at once. It appears in Approvals, and on the voucher's account page.

Seeding the first cohort

Make a business directly, and make platform vouch codes

Name, contact and owner. It stays Pending Activation until the owner verifies a contact, passes the NIN check, gives a trade name and accepts the Terms. No order is possible before that. The creator is recorded.

Past 75% of target

Escalated

Level 3 and NIN reviews both aim for 1 business day. After 75% the owner of the queue and the deputy are named, and the item shows in Escalated.

Checking an identity

Showing a NIN

Pick a reason first

Reasons: checking by hand, checking for a duplicate, a dispute, other with a note. The NIN is shown for as long as the page is open, and every show is a line in the record: who, when, why. Many shows in a day raise an alert.

Asking for a photo

"Send a photo of your NIN slip or ID card"

Proposed addition to Step 7: the applicant's Get approved screen shows this as a brass step only when Admin asks. Without it, the PRD gives Admin nothing to look at.

No NIN at all

Handled by phone, never from a screen

As decided in Step 7: no route in the app. Admin records an alternative check done by phone or WhatsApp (a passport or residence permit and the business's CAC number) on the account page, with a reason.

Three tries used

Resubmitting stops

He sees "Call or WhatsApp us". The account page shows the three outcomes and the reasons you gave.

Acting on an account

What each action does to him

Hold, revoke, disable, blocklist, vouching

Hold: a banner on every screen, no new orders, no refunds. Revoke: back to the Get approved screen, with prices hidden again. Disable a person: signed out and their saved data cleared at the next connection. Vouching paused: waiting codes stop working at once.

Finance started a hold

Ratify it within 1 business day

Finance can hold an account for payment fraud. It stays, and it waits in Approvals until you ratify or lift it.

Blocklist

NIN, contact, or a sending bank account

A NIN is kept only as a hash. A blocked item is refused at sign-up and at payment confirmation. The person is shown a neutral message, never why.

Acting as another role

You are acting as Finance

Any Admin use of another role's power, such as confirming a payment, shows this line first and is marked for the founder's weekly review.

Deciding a return

You investigated it

Someone else decides this one

The decide button is off and says why. When Operations investigated, Admin decides. When Admin investigated, Operations decides.

Other reasons, other options

The same page, a different list

Damaged on the way or wrong item: replacement, otherwise a credit note. Warranty: the maker's process only, and never a cash refund. Counterfeit confirmed: a refund by bank transfer. Change of mind: a credit note less 15%. Non-delivery: a new dispatch, or a refund.

Counterfeit decisions

Confirm or clear within 14 days

Confirmed needs a maker's serial check or two people with photos. A hold on a whole product needs a second approver.

Freight

Who pays is recorded on every return

If the fault is ours (our error, transit damage, dead on arrival, or counterfeit) we pay back actual courier cost up to the delivery rate for the same route. Otherwise the business pays.
