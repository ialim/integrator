Step 26A of the walk-through

Finance: the Platform Rate, credit note requests, and credit notes and refunds

Proposal for your reaction, in the Direction A look. Step 24 found three items on Finance’s rail with no screen: the Platform Rate, Credit note requests, and Credit notes and refunds. This sheet draws them, in the same console shell as Steps 13 to 16. Two days are used so the numbers agree with what you confirmed: Monday 12 October for the rate (Step 16 approves the same change), and Thursday 8 October for the credit notes (Step 9 is the business side of the same requests). Six desktop frames, then a sheet of states. Wording is placeholder where it is not quoted from the PRD. Nothing here is decided until you confirm it.

States

Brass cards need something from someone. Red cards mean it did not work or is refused. Plain cards are information. Each says what happened and what to do, in the console’s short, imperative voice.

Refund refused: credit only

A cancelled order cannot be refunded

Credit notes for cancellations, change-of-mind returns and uncollected pick-ups are credit only. The Owner’s app does not offer a refund for them.

Refund refused: on hold

This business is on hold

No refunds are paid while a hold lasts. Payments are still checked. The request stays here until the hold is lifted.

Refund above ₦100,000.00

Above the set amount, a refund or a credit note needs two people. It goes to Approvals and you see “Waiting for a second person”.
