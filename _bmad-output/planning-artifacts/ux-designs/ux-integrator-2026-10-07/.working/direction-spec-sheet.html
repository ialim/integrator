Spec Sheet

Spec Sheet speaks in the idiom of the trade: a monospaced face for quantities, codes and amounts, fine dashed rules, corner marks and one cyan line colour on white. Density is medium on a strict grid, so quantity, unit price and total line up like a bill of materials. Status is a short bracketed code with a symbol, such as [IN STOCK], which reads at a glance and never depends on colour.

Precise, technical and practitioner to practitioner: an installer's drawing sheet.
