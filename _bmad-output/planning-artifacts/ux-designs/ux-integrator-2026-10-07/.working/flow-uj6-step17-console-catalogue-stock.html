Step 17 of the walk-through

The staff console, Operations: products, stock, goods in, and the bulk upload

Proposal for your reaction, in the Direction A look. Same console shell. This is the catalogue side of Operations: the list of products, one product and what it needs before it can go live, stock and the record of goods coming in, and loading many products from a spreadsheet. Four desktop frames, then a sheet of states. Documentation, suppliers, stores and zones come in the next step. Wording is placeholder where it is not quoted from the PRD. Nothing here is decided until you confirm it.

States

Brass cards need something from someone. Red cards mean it did not work or is blocked. Plain cards are information. Wording is a placeholder.

Products

Cannot go live

Make it active is off, and the checklist says why

It stays off until the serialised answer, the Warranty Period (or None), the four returnability answers and any compliance reference are in. The button names what is missing.

SKU

Unique, and fixed once saved

A SKU cannot be edited or reused. A duplicate is refused with a link to the product that has it.

Admin's fields

Markup and Active Supplier are shown and locked

An Operations person sees them with a lock and "Admin only". A bulk file that fills them in has those columns ignored, with a note on each row.

Discontinued

Stays on projects that already hold it, with a warning

It is not on sale and cannot be added to a new project or cart.

Suspected or quarantined

Cannot be added, and every project that has it shows a warning

Marking a product or a batch Suspected is Operations or Admin's, and a hold on a whole product needs a second approver. It stops its sale, quarantines stock, blocks payment confirmation and shipment of affected lines, and tells everyone affected. Clearing it puts it back on sale.

Supplier cost

Zero or less is refused

The Active Supplier cannot be removed without a replacement, and only an Admin switches it. A switch shows how many carts and projects it touches.

Back-order discount

Above 10% needs an Admin, and below the floor is refused

Changing a discount or a lead time never changes an order line already made.

Stock and goods in

Below what is reserved

On hand cannot go below reserved

The save is refused and says how many are reserved and for which orders. Only an Admin can override.

A big adjustment

Above the set size, an Admin signs it off

It waits in Approvals. Until then the stock does not change. The set size is a rule Admin controls.

Safety stock

Units at or below the threshold are held back

"Release safety stock to back-order" makes them available to a back-order line. It is a ledger line with a reason.

Serial numbers on goods in

A repeat, a wrong count, or a wrong format

"NG4P-77124 is already dispatched on INT-0038." "29 entered, 30 needed." "NG4P-7712 is too short." Nothing is saved until the list is right.

A back-order line arrives

Mark it received only with a goods-in record

The line carries "ordered from supplier" and "received" markers. It cannot be marked received without a goods-in record, so stock and the promise stay true.

The books agree

On hand always equals the ledger

A daily check compares the two. If they ever differ, it shows on Today for Operations and for Admin, never silently.

Bulk upload

The template

Download it from the page, with a version number

A file made from an old template is told which columns changed. SKU is the match key. A missing or repeated key fails the row.

Waiting for Admin

A banner on Products and Bulk upload

"Catalogue file 12 Oct is waiting for an Admin. Nothing has changed yet." It also appears under Approvals for Admin.

After approval

Applied, with a one-click rollback

The previous state of every touched product is kept. Rolling back puts it all back, and writes both actions to the record.

A file that cannot be read

Wrong type, empty, or over the size limit

Said in one line before the dry run. The 500-row launch file must load in a single upload.
