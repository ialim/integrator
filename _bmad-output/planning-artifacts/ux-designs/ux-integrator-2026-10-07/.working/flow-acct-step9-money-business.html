Step 9 of the walk-through

Account: credit notes, loyalty tier, and the business profile

Proposal for your reaction, in the Direction A look. This is Tunde's account, the Owner of Adeyemi Integrated Systems. Six phone screens, two reference tables, then a sheet of states. Credit notes are the platform's alternative to a wallet: our finance team issues them, they last 12 months, and they can only be used to buy from us. Naira amounts use the sample rate of ₦1,368 to the dollar from Step 4. Wording is placeholder where it is not quoted from the PRD. Nothing here is decided until you confirm it.
