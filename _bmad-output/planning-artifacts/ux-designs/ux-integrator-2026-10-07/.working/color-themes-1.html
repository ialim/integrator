integrator: five colour themes, light mode

Five palettes that differ in feel, not only in hue. Compare the phone screen, the status chips and the staff queue in each one. Nothing here is decided.

  • Light mode only. English only. Amounts are in naira with VAT included.
  • Ratios are WCAG 2.2 contrast ratios. The minimum is 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for borders, icons and focus rings. Every pairing shown passes.
  • Status is never shown by colour alone. Every chip has a text label and an icon.
  • Hex values for each theme are in the style comment at the top of this file.
