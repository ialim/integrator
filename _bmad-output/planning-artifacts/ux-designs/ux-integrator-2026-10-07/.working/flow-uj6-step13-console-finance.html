Step 13 of the walk-through

The staff console, and Finance's day: confirming a payment

Proposal for your reaction, in the Direction A look. The console is desktop only and organised around work queues. This is UJ-6: Ngozi in Finance starts her day, opens the oldest receipt, matches it to the bank statement, and confirms. Five desktop frames, then a sheet of states. The look is the same as the app, denser: tables, a side panel, and no decoration. Role-aware navigation shows each person only their own queues and tools. Wording is placeholder where it is not quoted from the PRD. Nothing here is decided until you confirm it.

States

Brass cards need something from someone. Red cards mean it did not work or is blocked. Plain cards are information. Wording is a placeholder.

The shell and the day

Staff sign-in

Email, a code or passkey, then a second check

Staff must use two-step sign-in, and a session lasts 12 hours. The rail and the tools shown depend on the role. Finance never sees Operations' catalogue tools, and nobody sees a link they cannot use.

Shown on Today and above the Receipts queue. The "Import the latest" button sits inside it.

The Receipts queue

Over target

Target exceeded

Outside Business Hours

Uploaded Tue 5:40 pm, counted from Wed 8:00 am

A receipt sent after hours does not count against the two-hour target until the next opening. The row says so.

Sent back before

Sent back once before

A re-upload keeps its first upload time if the full amount matches. After 3 sent back on one order, a staff review is required and the row says "Needs review".

Sender is different

The sender is not the business

Confirm is replaced by "Send to a second reviewer…". The second reviewer cannot be the first. Both names are recorded.

Not your call

You created, approved or vouched for this business

Confirm is switched off. Another person must confirm. The reason is written in the check list, never hidden.

Order already expired, cancelled or paid

This order expired on 6 Oct

The receipt and any matching credit go to Unmatched bank credits, to be resolved with a credit note. Nothing is applied automatically.

Sent 26 h after the order was made

Confirming still works. The order is repriced once, when every in-time receipt has a decision, and the preview shows the new amount due at the new rate.

Reverse a payment

Reverse with a reason and a second approver

The order goes back to Awaiting Payment, and approval and shipping stop until it is reviewed. The ledger gets a new line. Nothing is edited.

After confirming

"INT-0042 is Paid. It is now in Operations' queue."

A short message with the ledger line number, and the next receipt opens. A setting turns "open the next one" off.

Keyboard and reading

Keyboard

Move with keys, decide with a button

J and K move down and up the list, Enter opens, / searches. Confirm, unconfirmed and reverse are never a single key. They need the button, or Enter on the focused button, and a summary line.

Dense, but readable

13 px tables, 44 px targets for buttons

Table text is smaller than the app's. Buttons and chips stay large enough to hit. Colour is never the only signal: every check says Matches, Clear, or Earlier in words.
