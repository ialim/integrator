Proposal for your reaction, in the Direction A look. These are the "You" rows from Step 8, as Tunde (the Owner of Adeyemi Integrated Systems) sees them. Six phone screens, a table that shows where every message lives in the app, then a sheet of states. This step also settles whether the app needs an in-app inbox. Wording is placeholder where it is not quoted from the PRD. Nothing here is decided until you confirm it.

Every message, and where you see it in the app

This is the answer to "does the app need an inbox?". The PRD sends 12 kinds of message by WhatsApp and email. In each case the same news already has a home on the screen it is about, so nothing lives only in WhatsApp or email. Anything that needs the person goes on Home under "Needs your attention". The recipients and channels are proposed here and still to be set in the PRD's notification matrix.
