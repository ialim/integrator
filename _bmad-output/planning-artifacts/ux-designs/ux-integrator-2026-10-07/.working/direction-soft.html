Soft

Soft treats the work as calm and approachable: large radii, white cards on a pale lilac ground, pill-shaped labels and one indigo accent. Density is medium, with one tile and one product thumbnail per item, and the Project total and both main actions grouped in a single card. Money is large and friendly, with the kobo set smaller but never hidden.

Calm, friendly and modern: a money app made for tradespeople.

Hero screen: Project, three variants

Phone frames are 360 px wide and shown at full scroll length. The lilac notches on the bezel mark 780 px, the bottom of a typical phone screen.

Checks for this direction

Lowest body-text contrast: 7.68:1 (caution text #6A3F00 on caution tint #FCEBC6), against the 4.5:1 AA minimum for normal text. Every text and background pairing in this file was computed from the final styles. Lowest control-border contrast: 4.20:1 (control border #6C679C on indigo tint #E9E5FA); lowest icon contrast: 6.81:1; both against the 3:1 minimum for UI components.

Touch targets on the phone frames are at least 44 x 44 px (stepper buttons 44 x 44, section toggles 44 x 44, buttons 52 to 60 px tall). Every status carries its word, and most also carry an icon. The focus ring is a 3 px indigo outline with a 3 px gap (8.39:1 on white, 7.55:1 on the screen ground). Light mode only.
