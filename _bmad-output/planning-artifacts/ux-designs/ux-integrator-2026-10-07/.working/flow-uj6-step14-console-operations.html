Step 14 of the walk-through

The staff console, Operations: approving, dispatching, and looking after a faulty device

Proposal for your reaction, in the Direction A look. Same console shell as Step 13, now for Operations. Bola approves a paid order after a stock check and dispatches it with serial numbers (UJ-6). Amaka, who holds the Support duties, takes a call about a faulty camera, makes the issue code, and checks the device when it arrives (UJ-7). Five desktop frames, then a sheet of states. Support duties are shown as a group inside Operations, as the PRD says, so a separate Support role can be split out later. Wording is placeholder where it is not quoted from the PRD. Nothing here is decided until you confirm it.

States

Brass cards need something from someone. Red cards mean it did not work or is blocked. Plain cards are information. Wording is a placeholder.

Approving an order

A line is not on the shelf

Credit this line

The line is cancelled, its reservation is released, a credit note is requested from Finance, and the business is told. The rest of the order is approved.

Every line missing

The order becomes Cancelled

With a credit note for the full confirmed amount. An order is never left Paid with no shipments.

Back-order line

Mark ordered from supplier

Supplier orders are placed only after payment. Operations marks "ordered from supplier" and later "received", and a line cannot be marked received without a goods-in record.

Ship in full

In-stock lines held for up to 14 days

After 14 days from approval the business is offered shipping in parts. The queue shows the day count on the line.

Change request before approval

New address, receiver, store, or parts or full

Shown at the top of the order with the old and new values side by side. A change never alters goods prices. A different zone changes only the delivery charge.

Cancel request after approval

Shipments are frozen until you decide, within 1 business day

Consent or refuse. A back-order line already ordered from the supplier cannot be cancelled. Shipped goods follow the returns rules.

Over target

Over 75% of 4 business hours

Escalated to Admin. The row shows "Escalated", and the rail count turns brass.

Shipments

Wrong jump

Processing cannot go straight to Delivered

Only the allowed moves are offered. Undoing a move is Admin only, with a reason.

Serial number problems

Already dispatched, wrong count, or wrong format

"NG4P-77124 was dispatched on INT-0038." "6 entered, 7 needed." "This does not fit the NG4P format." A correction needs a reason and a second person.

Pick-up

Ready for pickup only when the goods are at the store

Signed for records the collector's name and the type and last four digits of their ID. Reminders at 7 days, Uncollected at 14, cancel and restock after 30 days with a credit-only credit note less the restocking fee.

Delivery failed, lost, returned

Delivery failed goes back to Processing for another dispatch

Lost opens a non-delivery case by itself, and the order cannot be Completed while a shipment is lost or failed.

Dispatch store

The Main Store unless you pick another

A different store is chosen on the shipment. The store's address and hours are shown to the business for pick-ups.

The support desk

Caller not verified

Order and serial details stay hidden

Until the caller reads out the in-app code, or you call back the verified phone. Nothing about a serial or an order is shown before that. Both ways are recorded.

Serial not found

This number was never dispatched by us

No issue code can be made. Open a serial exception, which Operations and Admin both approve.

Third code, then stop

1 of 3 codes used for this order line

A new code after a lapse keeps the first case time. The fourth is refused.

WhatsApp

Only through the platform's WhatsApp Business account

Never a personal number. The conversation is attached to the case.

Returns

Serial does not match

Rejected at once, and the account is flagged

The serial scanned is not the one on the issue code. The business sees a plain reason. The flag is for Admin only.

Arrived after it lapsed

Held

The device came after the issue code ran out. Support contacts the business before anything else happens.

No fault found

It goes back at the business's cost

The cost is added to their next amount due, or taken from an open credit note, and shown in the decision.

I think it is fake

Flag it with evidence

Operations or Admin can mark a product or batch as Suspected. That stops its sale, quarantines stock, flags orders and proposals, and tells everyone affected. A whole-product hold needs a second approver.

Reclassify the reason

"Transit damage" becomes "dead on arrival"

With a note. It re-checks the window, exclusions and remedy, and the business sees the change.

Waiting for the maker

RMA number, date sent, date back

For repairs and warranty claims. The business sees "Waiting for the maker", never the RMA detail. A warranty claim never ends in a cash refund.

Time to investigate

2 business days from arrival

The queue shows the due time. Past 75%, it goes to Admin.
