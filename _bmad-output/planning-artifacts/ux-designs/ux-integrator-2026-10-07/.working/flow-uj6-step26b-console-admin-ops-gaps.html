Step 26B of the walk-through

Admin and Operations: holds, Terms, the first businesses, and delivery prices

Proposal for your reaction, in the Direction A look. These are the last four console screens the coverage check found missing. Holds and blocklist, the screen where Admin publishes a new version of the Terms, the screen where Admin creates the first businesses and makes platform vouch codes, and the small queue where Operations answers a business that asked for a delivery price to an area we do not serve yet. Same shell as Steps 13 to 16. Four desktop frames, then a sheet of states. Two rail items are new: Create a business under People for Admin, and Delivery price requests under Work for Operations. Wording is placeholder where it is not quoted from the PRD. Nothing here is decided until you confirm it.

States

Brass cards need something from someone. Red cards mean it did not work or is refused. Plain cards are information. Wording is a placeholder, and the hold, blocklist and Terms wording is on the counsel register.

Holds and blocklist

A Finance hold waiting for you

Ratify it within 1 business day

Finance can hold an account for payment fraud. It stays on hold. You ratify it, or lift it with a reason. If nobody acts in time it is escalated and shown on Today.

RatifyLift…

Lifting a hold

Lift the hold on Greenfield Security Ltd?

They can make new orders again, and refunds can be paid. A reason is required. They are told, unless the hold was a flagged fraud review, in which case you choose whether to tell them.

Someone on the blocklist signs up

What they see

“We could not create your account. Please call or WhatsApp us.” Never why. The refusal is counted on the entry, and Admin sees it in the audit trail.

A blocked account pays

The payment is held for review

A bank credit from a blocklisted account is not applied. It goes to Unmatched credits for Finance, marked blocked, and Admin is told.

Adding a NIN

Typed once, then only a hash

The number is turned into a hash at once and cannot be shown again. The list shows the last four digits and the hash. Every NIN read or added is a line in the audit trail.

A hold on a business with open orders

Payments are still processed

Orders already made carry on to approval and delivery. They just cannot make new ones, and no refund is paid. The business sees a banner with a number to call.

Terms

Counsel’s approval missing

Publish stays off

Add the date counsel approved the version, and a reference. It goes on the record with the version.

Published

Terms v4 is live

0 of 10 Owners have accepted. Each Owner sees the screen when they next open the app. You can remind them, and see who has not accepted.

Remind the Owners

An Owner has not accepted

Only creating an order is stopped

They see “Accept the new Terms to make an order”. Everything else works. Drawn in Step 10, screen 4.

Withdrawing a version

Only before anyone has accepted it

After the first acceptance a version cannot be taken back. A mistake is fixed by publishing the next version. Every version stays on record with its acceptances.

Creating businesses

Contact already registered

This phone already belongs to a business

It cannot be used for a second one. Open that business, or use a different contact.

NIN already used

Sent to manual review as a duplicate

The NIN belongs to another business. The new business waits in NIN reviews. Nothing is blocked, and nothing is shown to the Owner except “We are checking”.

Link not used

The link ran out after 7 days

Send the link again. The old one stops working. The business stays Pending Activation, and nothing about it changes.

A platform vouch code

Recorded as made by the platform

The code names the prospect, works once, and expires after 48 hours. When it is used, Admin reviews the Level 3 request as for any other code, with the platform as the voucher.

Delivery price requests

Not answered in time

Over 75% of 1 business day

It turns brass in the list and shows on Today. After 75% of the target it is escalated to Admin, as for every other queue.

We cannot deliver there yet

The reason goes to the business

A short reason the business will read, for example “We do not have a courier for Kano yet.” They are offered Collect from the Main Store.

The price was used

Fixed for that order

The order is made at ₦14,500.00 for delivery. Once the business has paid it cannot go up. If the courier charges more, the extra is ours and is reported to Finance.

The price ran out

Good until Fri 9 Oct, 6:00 pm

After that the business is told it has expired and can ask again or collect instead. The request moves to Answered, marked expired.
