Step 25B of the walk-through

Cancelling and changing an order

Proposal for your reaction, in the Direction A look. UJ-1 says Tunde cancels the order if his client withdraws before approval, and UJ-4 says he can cancel a back-order line that is more than 7 days late. Until now there was only an “Ask to cancel” button. Six screens follow an order through its life: paid and waiting for approval, the cancel confirmation and its result, a change to the delivery, a request to cancel once approved, and a late back-order line. A table says what a person can do at each point, and a sheet gives the other states, including the delivery exceptions. Nothing here is decided until you confirm it.

What a person can do, at each point in an order’s life

One table the build can test, and the app’s Orders screen follows it. Owners and Managers only. A Technician sees none of these actions, and their order links say “Ask Tunde”.
