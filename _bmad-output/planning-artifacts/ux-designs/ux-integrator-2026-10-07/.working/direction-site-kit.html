Site Kit

Site Kit borrows from safety signage and job-site labels: black ink, one hazard yellow, heavy type and thick outlines. It is the least dense of the four directions and has the largest touch targets, so it reads in glare and works with one hurried thumb. Status and money are loud: stock status is a solid label with an icon, and every total sits on its own slab.

Blunt, rugged and loud: a trade tool made to be read in sunlight.

Hero screen: Project, three variants

Phone frames are 360 px wide and shown at full scroll length. The yellow notches on the bezel mark 780 px, the bottom of a typical phone screen.

Checks for this direction

Lowest body-text contrast: 5.97:1 (secondary text #2B2A26 on desk #D8D6CB is lower than ink; the weakest pairing is the ok green #0B5A2A on concrete #E2E0D6), against the 4.5:1 AA minimum for normal text. Lowest control-border contrast: 12.74:1 (black #0A0A0A on hazard yellow #FFC800), against the 3:1 minimum for UI components.

Touch targets on the phone frames are at least 50 x 56 px. Every status carries its word plus an icon. The focus ring is a 4 px black outline with a 2 px gap (19.8:1 on white); it turns hazard yellow on black bars.
